Game guide

The idea

Practise mental arithmetic with darts scoring. Work out the answer in your head, type it on the keypad and tap ✓.

Reading a dart

S7 is a single 7 = 7. D7 is a double = 7 × 2 = 14. T7 is a treble = 7 × 3 = 21.

Question types

  • Dart total: add up the darts.
  • Countdown: take the throw away from what you need.
  • Checkout: take away what you have thrown, then halve what is left to find the double that finishes.
  • Doubles & trebles mode uses only D and T darts, and adds "What is T14 worth?" and "Which treble is worth 51?"

Levels

5 correct answers in a row moves you up a level. 3 wrong in a row drops you one. You can also tap a level (1 to 5) yourself.

Hints and speed

Tap Hint for the next step. Quick correct answers earn bonus points, up to 5 × your level.

60-second round

Tap 60s, then Start. Answer as many as you can. Hints are off, and the clock pauses while feedback shows. Your best round is saved for each level and mode.

Saved scores

Bests and your last level are stored on this device only. The home screen app and the browser keep separate scores.

Add to Home Screen on iPhone

  1. Open the game's link in Safari. Other browsers can't add it the same way.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you can't see it, tap More or Edit Actions.
  4. Keep the name Dart Sums and tap Add.
  5. Open the new icon on your home screen to play full-screen.

Menus can look slightly different between iOS versions.

Add to Home Screen on Android

Chrome

  1. Open the game's link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app).
  4. Confirm with Add or Install. The icon appears on your home screen or in your app list.

Samsung Internet

  1. Open the link and tap the ☰ menu.
  2. Tap Add page to, then Home screen.

Menu names vary a little between phones and browser versions.
